Standalone codec R&D
vs standard compressors.

Experimental standalone deterministic codec — not yet wired into Doctor or MonoFS runtime (stream.Writer/stream.Reader is the planned integration seam). 1 GB enwik9 & Small-Batch Numbers

Out-of-domain & streaming results

On the 1 GB Hutter Prize corpus (enwik9, trained on a 32 MB enwik8 prefix), nzip (H=32) finished in 2m 41s at 2.1 GB RSS (6.2 MB/s):

  • nzip: 249,465,417 B (0.2495) — smaller than bzip2 -9 (254.0 MB) and gzip -9 (322.6 MB).
  • Reference gap: xz -9e reaches 211.8 MB and the 2026 Hutter record fx2-cmix-T reaches 100.4 MB (2.48×) because natural-language prose rewards order 4–8 contexts.
  • Small telemetry batches: session mode packs 64×2 KB batches into 7.2 KB (vs 18.2 KB independent blocks); an 8 KiB dictionary compresses a 50 KB stream to 4.0%; a 137 KB gradient BMP packs to 87 bytes.

Current Bottlenecks & Saturation Limits

Where the stack currently hits physical ceilings

  • Doctor OTLP ingest ceiling (~43.8k rec/s): Sustains 20,000 rec/s at p95 34 ms and saturates near 43,800 rec/s (p95 210.7 ms) bound by downstream ClickHouse write throughput.
  • 1 MiB large-blob tail latency: Range-cached 1 MiB reads run at 192 ops/s (192 MB/s, p95 149 ms), while stale missing .state blobs can still produce p99 outliers on cold S3 lookups.
  • Scoped federation cap (32 targets/query): Live multi-account fan-out caps at 32 targets per query and 64 global inflight requests; broad unscoped fleet queries return HTTP 422 by design.
